## 1.6 Organization of the Project Book

The remainder of this report is organized as follows.

Chapter 1 introduces the project, providing context on the group music recommendation problem, a formal problem statement, the system objectives, scope and limitations, and the development methodology adopted.

Chapter 2 presents a survey of relevant literature, covering retrieval-augmented generation, hypothetical document embeddings, LLM-based song tagging, vector databases, and music recommendation systems.

Chapter 3 describes the system design and implementation, encompassing the three-service architecture, data enrichment pipeline, RAG inference workflow implementation, and deployment infrastructure.

Chapter 4 presents the system evaluation, including experimental setup, quantitative results, comparative analysis against existing approaches, and an interpretation of findings.

Chapter 5 concludes the report, summarizing the key technical contributions and proposing directions for future development.

Chapter 6 lists all references cited in the report, formatted according to the IEEE standard.

Appendix A provides step-by-step instructions for running all three JamOn services in a local development environment.
